Sentinel is an real-time traffic incident detection app focusing primarily on busy roads and high/freeways. Powered by AI, it aims to cut the delay between a crash happening and emergency responders being notified, as even mere seconds could mean life or death in critical situations. Sentinel is structured in three layers: a perception layer using motion detection, YOLOv8 object recognition, and BoT-SORT tracking; an event-understanding layer that converts raw detections into incident signals; and a cloud reasoning layer that uses vision-language models and a RAG pipeline over policy documents for semantic analysis. By only pushing incident-likely frames into the cloud reasoning layer, we were able make processing faster and cheaper. Another major engineering challenge was tuning the system for messy real-world video (glare, shadows, distant vehicles) and balancing inference cost against detection accuracy while minimizing false positives.
