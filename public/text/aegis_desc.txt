Awarded Best Use of Statistics during PennAppsXXVI, Aegis was inspired by my team and I's shared experiences living in big cities, where commuting by foot becomes increasingly dangerous at night. Using a combination of real-time crime data from police databases/APIS and satellite-derived street lighting information, Aegis computed Pareto-optimal paths using a multi-heuristic A* search algorithm. Users are able to choose between the path that is fastest, brightest or the best balance of the two, all without compromising on safety. The biggest engineering challenge was preventing "label explosion" in the path-planning algorithm at city scale, which we solved primarily with dominance pruning.
