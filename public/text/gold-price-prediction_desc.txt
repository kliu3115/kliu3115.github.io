This project forecasts next-day directional movement of the VanEck Gold Miners ETF (GDX) rather than raw gold spot prices, since GDX captures operational and geopolitical risk factors alongside broader equity sentiment. From merged daily data on gold-related signals, geopolitical risk, the dollar index, 10-year TIPS, and the VIX, I engineered a wide feature set — miner-gold spreads, short-horizon volatility, RSI/Bollinger transforms, calendar seasonality, and cross-asset relative strength — while excluding raw price levels to avoid leakage. Tuned gradient-boosted trees and random forests achieve about 73% next-day directional accuracy and a 0.80 ROC-AUC across walk-forward validation folds, a sizable gap over naive baselines (48-51%). A simple long/flat trading rule based on model confidence outperformed buy-and-hold over the test period, though multi-day forecasts were considerably weaker and the backtest does not yet account for realistic transaction costs or slippage.
