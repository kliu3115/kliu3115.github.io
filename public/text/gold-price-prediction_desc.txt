Combining a variety of Gold Miners ETF (GDX) related datasets from Kaggle, I merged the datasets and created new features like miner-gold spreads, rolling volatility, calendar effects, and more. I set out to predict whether or not GDX would have a positive return tomorrow, rather than the exact GDX return. Because financial data is time-dependent, I used walk-forward validation to train the models. Although I tested a variety of models, I found XGBoost to be the most promising and chose to spend most of my resources tuning its hyperparameters. To convert my model into a potential trading strategy, I did confidence-threshold testing and backtested its economic performance. The resultant model achieve 73.0% accuracy and 0.80 AUC on held-out next-day GDX direction, substantially outperforming linear/naive baselines. By applying confidence thresholds, I was able to increase precision to 83% while selectively reducing market exposure; my resulting long/flat strategy outperformed buy-and-hold in the backtest.
